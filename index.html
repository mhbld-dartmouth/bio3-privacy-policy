Privacy Policy

BIO3 · a mindfulness app from the MHBLD Lab at Dartmouth

Last updated · May 27, 2026

The BIO3 app ("BIO3," "we," "our," "us") is owned and operated by the Media, Health, and Behavior Lab in Dartmouth (MHBLD Lab) at Dartmouth College. This policy explains what information BIO3 collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

About the app

BIO3 is a mindfulness tracking app used as part of a Dartmouth College course. All students enrolled in the course use the app as part of their coursework. A subset of students may separately and voluntarily opt in to having their de-identified data used for academic research by the MHBLD Lab. Opting in or out of research has no effect on course participation or grading.

Who can use BIO3

BIO3 is intended for Dartmouth College students who are 18 years of age or older and enrolled in the associated course. The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Information we collect

BIO3 collects the following information that you provide directly:

  • Account information: your name, Dartmouth email address (which contains your NetID), and a password. Passwords are stored only in hashed form on our servers; we never see your plaintext password.
  • Course group assignment: the practice group you are assigned to within the course.
  • Daily meditation logs: date and time of submission, meditation duration, a self-rated quality score (1–5), and a self-rated stress level (0–10).
  • Group practice records: activity type, date, start and end times, duration, and optional notes for each group practice session you log.
  • Weekly screen time entries: a self-reported average of daily screen time (hours and minutes) for the prior week, and an optional screenshot you choose to upload from your device's Screen Time settings as supporting evidence.
  • Weekly reflections: short free-text reflections you choose to submit.

BIO3 also generates and stores limited technical information needed to operate the app, such as submission timestamps (used to detect late submissions against Eastern Time deadlines) and your time zone.

Information we do not collect

  • BIO3 does not access your location, contacts, calendar, microphone, photo library, or health data.
  • BIO3 does not read your device's screen time automatically; the values you enter are typed in by you. If you choose to upload a screenshot, it is a single image you select; we do not access any other photos on your device.
  • BIO3 does not include advertising trackers or third-party analytics SDKs.

Notifications

If you grant notification permission, BIO3 sends local reminders (for example, daily log reminders and weekly submission deadlines) scheduled on your device. You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings without affecting other app functionality.

How we use your information

For all students (course use): Your data is used to operate the app and support course activities, including authentication, the practice leaderboard, group practice tracking, deadline and late-submission detection, personal progress views, and instructor oversight of coursework.

For research participants only: Students who separately opt in to research may have their de-identified data used for academic research and publications by the MHBLD Lab. Research data is only reported in aggregate or de-identified form in any publication.

Storage and security

Your data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored on encrypted servers operated for the MHBLD Lab. Access is limited to authorized lab researchers and course instructors at Dartmouth College. We maintain reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards against unauthorized access, use, modification, or disclosure. However, no data transmission over the Internet can be guaranteed to be perfectly secure.

Data sharing

We do not sell your data. We do not share your personal data with advertisers or third-party marketers. Research findings derived from BIO3 data are reported only in aggregate or de-identified form.

Data retention

Course-related data is retained for the duration of the course and as required by Dartmouth College record-keeping policies. Research data from opted-in participants may be retained in de-identified form for the duration permitted by the lab\'s IRB-approved protocol.

Your choices and rights

  • Stop using the app: you may stop using BIO3 at any time.
  • Withdraw from research: if you opted in to research, you may withdraw your research data at any time by contacting the lab. Withdrawal will not affect your course participation or grade.
  • Account deletion: you may request deletion of your account and associated data by emailing the developer contact below. Some records may be retained where required by course or institutional policy.

Links to other websites

BIO3 and our materials may contain links to websites we do not own or control. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites and encourage you to review their privacy policies.

Legal disclosure

We may disclose information we collect if required or permitted by law, such as to comply with a subpoena or similar legal process, or when we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, your safety, or the safety of others, to investigate fraud, or to respond to a lawful government request.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated through the app or by email to registered users, and the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be revised.
