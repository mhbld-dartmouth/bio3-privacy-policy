# Privacy Policy **BIO3 | Dartmouth College** **Last updated: November 20, 2026** BIO3 is a mindfulness and activity tracking application associated with Dartmouth College. This Privacy Policy describes how information is collected and used through the application. ### 1. Information We Collect BIO3 may collect information necessary to provide its features, including: - Account information, such as name, username, and email address. - Meditation logs, including duration, quality ratings, and stress ratings. - Weekly reflections, screen time entries, and uploaded content. - Group participation, activity progress, and submission records. - Basic technical information related to application operation. ### 2. How Information Is Used Information collected through BIO3 may be used to operate and maintain the application, manage user accounts, support course activities, track participation and progress, and address technical issues. Information may also be used for educational or research purposes where applicable and appropriately authorized. ### 3. Data Storage and Security BIO3 uses electronic systems and service providers to store and process information. Reasonable measures are used to protect information, although no electronic system can guarantee complete security. ### 4. Information Sharing Information may be accessible to authorized individuals involved in administering, maintaining, or supporting BIO3, as well as service providers involved in its operation. Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or institutional requirements. ### 5. Data Retention Information may be retained as necessary for application operations, educational activities, and applicable institutional or legal requirements. ### 6. Third Party Services BIO3 may use third party services for hosting, storage, authentication, and other technical functions. These services may process information necessary to support the application. ### 7. Changes to This Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. The latest version will be available through the application. Users may be asked to review and accept an updated version when changes are made. ### 8.
